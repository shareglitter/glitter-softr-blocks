Neighbor-funded block cleaning in Philly

A typical cleaning costs $50. Neighbors pledge what they can towards the shared cost.

Enter your address to make a pledge. No payment information is collected here.

Want to check first? Scroll down to the map. Blocks appear after the first pledge; if yours isn’t listed, you can be first to get it started then share the page we make for your block with your neighbors.

Once pledges are enough to fund a cleaning, we’ll email a Stripe invoice to pay by ACH or credit card. No payment is due before then.
